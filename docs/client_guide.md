# GPM: Client Portal Guide

Welcome! The GenArtML Client Portal is your dedicated space to track the progress of your project, see upcoming milestones, and communicate directly with the development team.

## 1. Accessing the Portal
- Navigate to the portal URL and click **Client Portal Login** at the bottom of the screen.
- You will need three pieces of information to log in securely:
  1. **Your Email** (where you will receive updates)
  2. **Project ID** (provided by your GenArtML manager)
  3. **Portal Password** (provided by your GenArtML manager)
- Once logged in, your session is secure and tied to your specific project.

## 2. Tracking Progress
The portal is divided into three main columns:
- **Completed Work**: Tasks that have been fully developed, tested, and delivered.
- **In Progress**: What the team is actively working on right now.
- **Future Work**: Upcoming tasks and features planned for the next phases of development.

Click on any task to see its full description and any updates provided by the team.

## 3. Submitting a Request or Ticket
If you need to report an issue, request a change, or ask a question:
- Click the **Submit New Request** button.
- Select a **Priority** (Low, Medium, High).
- Optionally, specify a **Deadline** if this request is time-sensitive.
- Provide a detailed description of what you need.
- Once submitted, you will immediately receive an email confirmation containing your Request ID.

## 4. Editing Requests
- Made a typo or forgot to add a detail? You can edit your requests directly from the portal while they are still marked as "Open".
- The team will automatically see that the request has been updated.

## 5. Receiving Updates
- You don't need to constantly check the portal to stay informed. 
- When the GenArtML team resolves one of your requests, you will automatically receive an email notification detailing the resolution.
