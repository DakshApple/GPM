# GPM: Team Member Guide

Welcome to the GenArtML Project Manager. Your workspace is streamlined to show you exactly what you need to focus on.

## 1. Logging In
- Navigate to the main portal URL.
- Enter the username and password provided to you by your Admin.
- *Note: If you need to change your password, click the 🔑 (Key) icon next to your name at the bottom of the sidebar.*

## 2. Your Workspace (Data Privacy)
- You will **only** see Projects, Tasks, and Client Tickets that belong to the projects you have been explicitly assigned to.
- If you believe you are missing a project, contact your Admin to have it added to your account.

## 3. Working with Tasks
- Go to the **Tasks** tab.
- You can filter tasks by "To Do", "In Progress", "Review", and "Done".
- To update a task's status, click on it and use the status dropdown, or click the **Advance Status** button.
- If a task is marked as "Client Visible", the client will be able to track its progress in their portal. Keep the client-facing description clear and professional.

## 4. Notifications
- When a client submits a new request for a project you are assigned to, you will receive an email notification.
- When an admin or manager resolves a client ticket on your project, you will receive a CC notification to keep you in the loop.

## 5. Quick Navigation
- Press `Cmd+K` (Mac) or `Ctrl+K` (Windows) to open the Command Palette.
- From here, you can instantly search for a specific project, jump to a different view, or quickly create a new task without leaving your current page.
